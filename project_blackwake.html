Flagship Enterprise GRC Lab

Project Blackwake

An enterprise governance, risk, compliance, and assurance simulation built around Bastion Harbor Industries, a fictional maritime, defense logistics, industrial services, and technology company.

View GitHub Repository

Project Thesis

Enterprise risk operates as one connected system.

Project Blackwake examines how business objectives, risks, controls, evidence, audit findings, vendors, contracts, personnel, technology, finance, continuity, and executive accountability influence one another across a complex organization.

Enterprise Scale

A full operating environment

The fictional company includes $382 million in annual revenue, 1,180 employees, 146 contractors, 214 vendors, 63 enterprise systems, 2,940 managed endpoints, and 418 operational technology assets.

Risk and Control Model

Structured assurance at enterprise depth

The lab contains 60 enterprise risks, 120 controls, 37 audit findings, and 20 initial warning signs. Each control connects to ownership, evidence, frequency, testing procedures, remediation deadlines, and business consequences.

Core Deliverables

Evidence built for review

Project Blackwake includes a corporate dossier, master enterprise workbook, risk register, control library, audit findings, vendor records, contract data, system inventories, executive reporting, and public CSV evidence.

Framework Architecture

Governance across multiple standards

The project incorporates NIST CSF 2.0, NIST RMF, NIST SP 800-53, NIST SP 800-53A, NIST SP 800-171, ISO/IEC 27001:2022, ISO 9001:2015, CMMC, and internal control concepts.

Analyst Perspective

Risk translated into executive action

The working logic follows the complete assurance chain: what objective is threatened, which control should reduce the risk, what evidence supports the control, what finding remains, who owns remediation, and what decision leadership must make.
