# Project Meridian: Enterprise AI Governance & Model Risk Lab

Project Meridian is a fictional enterprise Artificial Intelligence (AI) governance portfolio lab built to demonstrate how a Governance, Risk, and Compliance analyst can turn AI adoption into a controlled operating process.

The lab follows Meridian Services Group, a fictional 4,800-person enterprise using generative and predictive AI across customer service, human resources, security operations, marketing, legal support, and internal knowledge work.

## What the project demonstrates

- AI use-case inventory and ownership
- Risk classification and risk acceptance
- NIST Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF) operating logic
- Generative AI-specific risk analysis using NIST AI 600-1
- ISO/IEC 42001:2023 management-system concepts
- European Union Artificial Intelligence Act scoping lens
- Human oversight and consequential-decision safeguards
- Third-party and model-provider governance
- Model/system documentation
- Logging, monitoring, change control, and traceability
- AI incident response and reauthorization
- Executive reporting

## Governance lifecycle

**Intake → Classify → Assess → Approve → Monitor → Retire**

Every AI use case must have a named business owner, documented purpose, data classification, affected stakeholders, risk tier, human-oversight requirement, approval status, monitoring plan, and retirement condition.

## Note on framework currency

This lab uses the currently published NIST AI RMF 1.0 and NIST AI 600-1 as of September 2026. NIST states that AI RMF 1.0 is under revision, so the source file records that status rather than presenting the framework as static.
