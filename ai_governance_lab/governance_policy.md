# Meridian Services Group AI Governance Policy

## Purpose
Establish minimum governance requirements for Artificial Intelligence systems used by Meridian Services Group.

## Core requirements
1. Every business AI use case must be recorded in the enterprise AI inventory before production deployment.
2. Every use case must have a named business owner and technical or vendor-management owner where applicable.
3. AI systems must be risk-classified according to data sensitivity, affected stakeholders, decision consequence, automation level, external exposure, regulatory relevance, and dependency on third parties.
4. AI must not independently make consequential employment, financial, legal, safety, or similarly high-impact decisions unless specifically authorized by law and enterprise policy. A qualified human must retain meaningful review and override authority where required.
5. Sensitive data may only be processed through approved AI systems under documented data-use, retention, access-control, and contractual requirements.
6. Medium- and high-risk AI systems require documented testing appropriate to the use case.
7. Third-party AI providers must undergo risk-based due diligence.
8. Approved AI systems must be monitored according to risk tier; material changes trigger reassessment.
9. Suspected harmful, unauthorized, deceptive, insecure, discriminatory, privacy-impacting, or materially incorrect AI behavior must be reported.
10. Retired systems must have access revoked, integrations disabled, retained data handled according to policy, and the inventory updated.

## Risk acceptance
Residual AI risk above approved tolerance requires written acceptance by the designated risk owner and an expiration or review date.
