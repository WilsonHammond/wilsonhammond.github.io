# Human Oversight Plan

## Objective
Ensure AI-supported work preserves meaningful human accountability, especially where errors can materially affect people, money, rights, security, or legal obligations.

## Oversight levels
- **Level 1: Editorial review** for low-impact drafting and administrative support.
- **Level 2: Qualified validation** for analytical summaries, customer-facing drafts, security analysis, and internal guidance.
- **Level 3: Independent decision authority** for consequential decisions such as employment screening or high-impact financial recommendations.
- **Level 4: Restricted automation** when risk or law requires narrow automated behavior with explicit boundaries, tested controls, monitoring, override mechanisms, and formal approval.

## Minimum evidence
Named reviewer role, documented decision boundary, qualification requirement, override capability, escalation path, sampled review evidence, exception handling, and metrics for automation bias or over-reliance where relevant.

## Employment use case rule
For AI-003 Applicant Screening Support, AI output may rank or summarize according to approved job-related criteria, but it may not make the final hiring decision. Recruiters must independently review candidate qualifications.
